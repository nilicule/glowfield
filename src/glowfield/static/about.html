About Glowfield

A small logic puzzle about catching fireflies on a summer night.

Glowfield turns minesweeper inside out. You don't uncover cells and hope. Every number is already showing, and each one tells you how many fireflies are hiding in the eight cells around it. Your job is to work out exactly where they all are.

Double-click to catch a firefly Click once to rule a cell out Three lanterns, one per wrong catch

You never have to guess

Every board starts with a clue on every cell that isn't a firefly. Clues are then removed one by one, and after each removal a solver checks that the puzzle can still be finished by logic alone. If it can't, the clue goes back.

Hints use the same solver, so they can always show the next step and explain why it works.

Your data

There are no accounts, and the server doesn't store anything about you.

The only thing kept is a small cookie in your own browser that remembers you've solved today's daily puzzle, so it can show your result instead of a fresh board.

Difficulty

Levels differ in the kind of reasoning they ask for, not just in board size.

  • Beginner 6×6

    One clue at a time, with plenty of easy moves available at every point.

  • Easy 7×7

    Still one clue at a time, with fewer clues and fewer obvious moves.

  • Medium 8×8

    Sometimes you compare two clues, where one's cells sit inside the other's.

  • Hard 9×9

    Adds overlap reasoning: two clues share some cells, and one needs more fireflies than the other.

  • Expert 10×10

    Leans on overlap reasoning again and again.

  • Daily 8×8

    One Medium board a day, the same for everyone. You can solve it once.
