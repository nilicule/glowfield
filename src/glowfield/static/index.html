How to play

  • Numbers count the fireflies hiding in the eight cells around them.
  • Double-click a cell to catch a firefly. Catch one where there isn't any and a lantern goes out. Lose all three and the night is over.
  • Click once to rule a cell out. That's just a note for yourself, so it's never checked. Click again to clear it.
  • Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone. Stuck? Hint shows the next step and explains why.
